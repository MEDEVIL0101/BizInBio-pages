Questions

Can Imorta see my photos?

No. Faces are recognised on your device, and backups are encrypted there before they're sent. We store scrambled files we have no key to.

What happens if I stop paying for Family?

Your archive stays on your devices and keeps working as Keepsake. The encrypted backup is kept for a grace period, then removed.

What happens to a Child Vault if I stop paying, or I'm not around?

It's frozen, not deleted: nothing new is added, and what's there is kept until your child is old enough to claim it. They, or another custodian, can take it up again.

How does my child claim their vault?

From you, if you're there: you hand over the key and they become the owner. If you're not, they claim it on this website by proving who they are, using two of the three key shares made when the vault was opened.

Why is there a one-off price for the vault?

Paying once to age 18 means the vault can't lapse, whatever happens to the person paying. That's the point of it.
