Stays on your device

Faces are found and matched on your own phone or in your own browser, and everything is kept there. Imorta stores nothing.

Photos and papers together

How it works

From a camera roll to a childhood.

  1. Tell Imorta who to look forAdd each child and their birthday. A photo from before they were born can't be them, which is how siblings are told apart.
  2. Show it your photosOn iPhone it reads your library. On the web you choose the photos or a folder. Either way they stay on your device.
  3. Point out your child onceImorta groups the faces it found. Tap the ones that are your child and it follows them through the years.
  4. Watch them growAn album by age, newest first. Milestones, growth, documents, and what happened on this day in years gone by.
